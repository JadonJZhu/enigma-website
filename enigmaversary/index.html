EnigmaVersary 40!

Hello Enigmans, Enigmaniacs, and Enigmata! We're excited to announce the 40-year anniversary of Enigma, scheduled for 2026!

Celebrating 40 Years of Enigma

Over the past 40 years, we have explored the worlds of science fiction, fantasy, horror, LARPs, tabletop RPGs, board games, and every kind of nerd- and geekdom at UCLA. We hope you will join us in 2026 to celebrate our past, present, and future.

🕒

When

MLK Day weekend
Friday Jan. 16 - Monday Jan 19, 2026

📍

Where

UCLA campus
Westwood, and central LA

🎲

What

Games, reminiscing, reunion, parties, and every kind of fun

View Events Calendar

Schedule Overview

  • Use the Events Calendar above and join our Discord for detailed information and questions.
  • On Friday, come to Ackerman Union for Open Board Gaming from 5 PM to 10 PM and Zombie Tag from 8 PM to 10 PM.
  • On Saturday, join us at an alumni's house at 12 PM for various events, including LARPs, social deception games, and more! Join our Discord for further details and to reserve your seat for transportation on our charter bus or a rideshare.
  • On Sunday, compete in our Chrono Agents Road Rally at (3 PM to 7 PM) in West LA, enjoy a group dinner at Cafe 50s (7:30 PM to 11:30 PM), and participate in tabletop games at Saxon Suites throughout the day. Join our Discord for sign up information.
  • On Monday, ice skate with us in Santa Monica at 11 AM to 3 PM and come to Saxon Suites starting at 3 PM for open board gaming, an original ROOT-themed LARP, and other party activites. Join our Discord for further details.

How to Participate

  • Show up to any of the events in our schedule (see above)! Shared Saturday transportation, Chrono Agents Road Rally, and ice skating are the only things requiring prior sign-up.
  • Fill out our Family Feud form.
  • Contact us at enigma.uclaofficers@gmail.com or on Discord to ask any questions.
  • Pull out your archives of photos, documents, flyers, and props from every era! Submit your artifacts by uploading to the following Google Drive

Get Involved!

  • Propose your own events for Sunday and Monday on the official event calendar. Also sign up for events on those days!
