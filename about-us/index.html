About Us

Meet the masterminds behind Enigma's adventures

Our Mission

From casual game nights to tournament-style competitions, from learning the basics of Dungeons & Dragons to exploring the streets of LA, Enigma provides the platform and community for you to level up your gaming and social experience while making lifelong friends along the way. We include everyone from undergrads to graduate students and alumni. If you're interested in board games, tabletop games, or anything nerdy (even just a bit), you're always welcome with us. There is no formal membership, so drop by any time! Students, staff, faculty, and local community members are all welcome!

Our mission is to create an inclusive space where players of all experience levels can gather, learn, and grow together. We believe that the best games happen when diverse perspectives come together around the table, creating memorable experiences that extend far beyond the final score.
